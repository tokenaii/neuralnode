product names are owned by TokenAI. The source-available license grants no
ownership or general trademark license.

## Permitted reference

You may make a factual reference to NeuralNode when acknowledging that your
product uses or is based on the framework, provided that you do not imply that
TokenAI endorses, sponsors, certifies, or maintains your product.

Forks and derivative products must use a distinct product name and must not be
presented as the official NeuralNode project. Do not use the NeuralNode logo or
TokenAI branding in a way that suggests official status.

Additional trademark use requires written permission from TokenAI.
